About me

A little more than a name and a degree.

I am a Physics undergraduate at Universitas Brawijaya with a curiosity that moves between the sciences, languages, education, and the creative arts.

Alongside my academic journey, I have spent more than three years exploring graphic design and photography, starting in high school.

Through student organizations, educational communities, and creative projects, I have learned to work with people, manage responsibilities, and turn ideas into something tangible.

I believe curiosity becomes more meaningful when it is shared — through thoughtful conversations, useful work, and opportunities to contribute to the people around us.
